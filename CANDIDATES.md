# Candidate lines — The One-Line Exit

Scored in this session against the judge's brief (most important first):
joke lands as a one-line resignation letter, surprise, fits the form
(reads like a letter someone could actually send), specific voice,
quotable/stays with the reader — plus concision. Each criterion 1–5.
All lines are one line, 1–140 characters.

## Winner: #10

> I hereby resign as the only person who knows where the files are, effective the moment you finish this sentence.

Why it won: it is the only candidate that scores top marks on every
judge criterion at once. The surprise is double — the leverage (only
person who knows where the files are) and the timing (the resignation
takes effect mid-sentence, while the reader is still reading it). The
voice is specific and instantly recognizable: the indispensable
employee every office has. It reads exactly like a letter someone
could send, with no padding and no list of grievances. And it is
quotable — a colleague would forward this one. The runners-up (#6,
#9, #11, #12) all land one joke but stay generic or restate a familiar
complaint; #10 turns the resignation itself into the punchline.
